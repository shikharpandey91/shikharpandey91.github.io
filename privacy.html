Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who uses this app

My Personal Assistant is a private tool. Only its owner uses it, with his own Google account.

What data the app reads

With the owner's consent, the app reads emails and attachments in the owner's Gmail account. It can also create draft emails in that account.

Where the data goes

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Email data stays on that computer and in the owner's Google account.

The app does not sell, share or send Gmail data to any other person or company. It does not use Gmail data for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
